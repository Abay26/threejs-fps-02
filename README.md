Meridian Yard is a round-based tactical FPS in three.js: two teams of five fight short rounds in a dusk industrial yard — plant the spike at Site A (Crane) or Site B (Depot), or eliminate the other team. Money between rounds buys rifles, SMGs, a sniper and Kevlar, and bots patrol, hold sites, plant and defuse.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 — click to play, WASD + mouse, E plants/defuses, B buys
```
